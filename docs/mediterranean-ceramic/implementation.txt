Scope
Login, REC Manager and Consumer. Jobs surfaces remain unchanged. The shared
AuthBackdrop keeps its original default images; login supplies its own image list.
Existing login accounts and credentials are unchanged.

Visual system
Warm ivory and sage, the original EnergAIze logo, generated ceramic illustrations,
uniform community headers and the existing shared React account menu. Three newly generated login
images rotate every nine seconds; reduced-motion preferences are respected.
The login has no pause control. The landscape fills the viewport, with text over the sky and the sign-in form
on the right on desktop. Ceramic plug transitions are shared
by login and logout.
Equipment selection updates only the scene, preserving mounted telemetry charts.
Responsive scenes display the entire image; hotspot coordinates are percentages
of that uncropped image. Visible illustration captions were removed at the user’s
request; image provenance remains recorded alongside this implementation.

Functional preservation
Overview delegates to the existing site and community measurement workspaces when
charts are expanded. Inventory, topology, settings, source configuration, runtime
services, charging plans and the flexibility calendar/forms remain available.
Equipment buttons use the existing manager/resident routes. Access filtering and
control permissions apply before equipment, images and flexibility actions render.
Topology uses containment and Ceramic icons instead of intersecting wires.
SVG exports embed their icon artwork and remain self-contained.

Image coverage
Residential: no exterior energy equipment; solar; battery; solar + battery;
solar + battery + EV charger. These cover the current residential catalog.
Apartment/commercial buildings and factories: neutral and all-three variants.
For other combinations, duplicate devices of one type, or unknown site types,
no inaccurate hotspot is shown. This is a curated generated image library;
there is no runtime image-generation service or claim of true site geometry.
Interior meters/appliances remain accessible in the equipment dock without an
invented outdoor location. The community image is conceptual, not a site map.

Data
Counts and equipment specifications come from the existing backend model.
Repeated equipment types have one dock icon and a device list; individual device
navigation, permissions and flexibility actions remain available. Missing specs
are not replaced with invented values. Site strips support mouse-wheel scrolling,
releasing page scrolling at either end. Scorecards use consistent internal padding
and four columns on wide screens, two columns on narrow screens. Existing
scorecard examples remain explicitly marked Illustrative values. Measurement
charts retain their existing sources, loading states and empty states.
